StreamDock is free. Keep it that way.

No licence key, no account, no telemetry, no ads — and none of that is going to change. It is one person's side project, built in the open under the MIT licence, and it stays free whether or not this page ever earns a shilling.

Sharing it helps more than money does. If you would like to chip in as well, there are a couple of ways below. Both are entirely optional.
